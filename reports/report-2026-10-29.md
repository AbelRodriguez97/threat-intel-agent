  4. Evaluate each asset's internet exposure and ensure adherence to BOD 26-04 patching guidelines.

#### Rationale

_The vulnerability is classified as medium because it involves insufficient logging rather than direct exploitability for initial access or privilege escalation. It requires existing administrative access to be leveraged, primarily impacting an organization's ability to detect and respond to actions taken by an already compromised administrator account, rather than facilitating the initial compromise itself. This type of vulnerability complicates forensic analysis but does not directly enable system takeover or data exfiltration without prior access._
